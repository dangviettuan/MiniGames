# Cờ vua

Open `#chess` in the game hub. This is an untimed local two-player game, without AI, accounts, or networking. The current game and its Undo/Redo history are saved automatically on the device.

## Rules and architecture

`src/chess/engine.js` is a pure immutable engine with no React/browser dependencies. Board indices run a8 to h1; pieces use uppercase for White, lowercase for Black. `initialState`, `fromFEN`, `legalMoves`, `makeMove`, `inCheck`, `outcome`, `drawClaims`, `claimDraw`, `makeMoveAndClaim`, and `resign` are exported.

Supported: legal movement and self-check filtering, check/checkmate/stalemate, both castling sides (unmoved king/rook, empty route, no check/transit/final attacks), en passant including discovered-check restrictions and expiration, all four promotion choices including captures, insufficient mating material, repetition, and move-count draws. Repetition keys include castling rights and only legally capturable en passant targets.

Threefold repetition and 50 moves per player without pawn movement/capture enable **Yêu cầu hòa** rather than ending immediately. The optional **Yêu cầu hòa nếu nước tiếp theo đủ điều kiện** control also claims atomically on an intended move that reaches either threshold. Fivefold repetition and 75 moves per player end automatically. Checkmate takes precedence over the move-count draw. Insufficient material recognizes kings alone, king plus a lone bishop/knight versus king, and bishops confined to the same square color. Two knights versus king are not automatically declared dead because a mating position remains possible with cooperation. Arbitrary blocked-board dead-position proofs are not implemented.

Resignation is a draw when the opponent has only a bare king, because no legal continuation can let that opponent checkmate. Otherwise resignation awards the opponent the game. This specifically handles the obvious bare-king case; it does not claim to solve all possible cooperative-mate restrictions involving minor pieces or blocked positions. Positions already recognized as insufficient material have already ended and cannot be resigned.

`src/games/chess/index.jsx` owns UI selection, promotion choice, orientation and the immutable timeline. Undo/Redo restores all rule state, repetition history, castling rights, en passant rights, promotions and terminal results. A new action after Undo truncates the future timeline. Resignation and new game require accessible modal confirmation. Cancelling a reset preserves the complete current timeline and redo tail. Confirming a reset starts a new timeline. Board labels, live status, keyboard-operable square buttons, marked legal destinations and scoped styles support touch/keyboard use. The shared board-first PlayLayout places controls beside the board in landscape and below it in portrait. Pieces scale with the actual board container, so short landscape screens retain a readable board without overflowing glyphs. Coordinate labels cannot intercept pointer events. Rules/history are collapsed; new game, board rotation, resignation and advanced draw preferences are under Tùy chọn.

`src/games/chess/state.js` validates saved snapshots by replaying the entire timeline from the standard initial position, including the redo tail, claims and resignation. This checks the board, turn, castling/en passant rights, move counters, repetition keys and terminal outcomes. Invalid JSON, incompatible versions or corrupted states fall back to a fresh game. Repetition history is stored once, with a prefix length per snapshot, to avoid quadratic saved data; existing uncompressed saves still load. Durable fields are `timeline`, `cursor`, `flipped` and `claimOnMove`; selection and pending promotion/confirmation UI are transient. If browser storage is blocked/full, the shared layout displays a warning while gameplay continues.

## Validation

`npm test` includes opening perft depth 3 (8,902), Kiwipete perft depths 1–3 (48 / 2,039 / 97,862), pins, illegal turns/movement, self-check, castling attacks/rights/rook capture, en passant expiration/discovered check, all promotions, Fool's Mate, stalemate, material draws, reached/intended-move claims, automatic draws and mate precedence.

`npx playwright test e2e/chess.spec.js` runs eleven real UI scenarios in each configured desktop, iPad and mobile project: legal/invalid actions, branching Undo/Redo, rotation/reset, full Fool's Mate and terminal recovery, legal opening to castling, en passant, legal pawn race to promotion with cancellation/underpromotion, repetition claim/resignation, responsive bounds/control visibility at 1366×768, 834×1194, 1194×834, 393×851, 360×640 and 851×393, actual landscape touch taps, reload/redo/preference retention, cancelled-reset retention and corrupt-save fallback. Castling, en passant, promotion and checkmate paths also reload to verify restored rights/results. The touch-only scenario is skipped on desktop profiles. `node --test src/games/chess/state.test.js` covers save replay/corruption, redo tails, terminal results, draw claims and promotion snapshots. No board injection or test-only production hooks are used.

Local browser validation uses Chromium for all three viewport/device profiles because Playwright browser CDN access is unavailable in this environment. The checked-in normal configuration retains the true WebKit iPad project for CI; local iPad Chromium results do not prove Safari behavior. No deployment is required to exercise this feature.
